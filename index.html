Before the visit

The decision can start before someone reaches your door.

A customer searches for coffee, lunch or baked goods nearby. They compare hours, photos, reviews and menus—often in a couple of minutes.

Your Google profile does not need to be flashy. It needs to be accurate, current and easy to trust. The audit shows you where that experience becomes confusing or incomplete.
